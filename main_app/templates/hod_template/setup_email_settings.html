Using Gmail? You'll need an "App Password", not your normal Gmail password:
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification on the Google account you want to send from, if it isn't already.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com → Security → App passwords.
  3. Create one (any name works) and copy the 16-character password it gives you.
  4. Host: smtp.gmail.com, Port: 587, Username: your full Gmail address, Password: the App Password, TLS: on.
