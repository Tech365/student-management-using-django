Privacy Notice

This is a general-purpose draft notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed and customized by {{ site_settings.school_name|default:"Madrasa Jamaliyah" }} before being treated as an official policy.

What we collect

This system stores information needed to run day-to-day attendance and academic administration:

  • Student and staff names, contact details, and addresses
  • A profile photo, if one is uploaded
  • Daily attendance records
  • Test and exam results
  • Leave requests and feedback submitted through the system
Why we collect it

This information is used solely to administer the Madrasa: recording attendance, tracking academic progress, managing staff and student records, and communicating with students and staff.

Who can see it

Access is restricted to Madrasa administrators and teaching staff, and is limited to what each role needs: teachers can see records for the students they teach, and students/parents can see only their own records.

How it's stored

Data is stored in a private database accessible only to authorized administrators, and is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Automated backups are kept for disaster recovery.

Questions

If you have questions about the information held about you or your child, please contact the Madrasa administration directly.
